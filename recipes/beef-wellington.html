Beef Wellington

Serves 8 Prep 40 min Cook 1½ hr Total 3 hr 40 min Equipment Butcher's twine · Cast-iron skillet · 12″ skillet · Small skillet · Food processor · Plastic wrap · Rolling pin · Rimmed baking sheet · Foil · Instant-read thermometer · Serrated knife
900 gBeef tenderloincentre-cut, trimmed · 2 lb
to tasteKosher salt & pepperliberally

Tie

Twine at 2.5 cm intervals. Season liberally.

2 TbspVegetable oil

Sear

Oil smoking, high heat. Brown each side without moving, ~2 min a side.

~10 min
2 TbspPrepared horseradish
1 TbspMustardDijon or hot English
1 tspBlack pepper

Coat

Mix horseradish, mustard and pepper. Once beef is cool, cut off twine and rub all over. Fridge, uncovered.

450 gMushroomsany mix, roughly chopped · 1 lb

Pulse

In two batches, ~10 short pulses each, until very fine.

55 gUnsalted butter4 Tbsp
2Shallotsfinely diced
2 tspFresh thymefinely minced

Brown

Medium-high until liquid is gone, then until fond sticks. Add shallots and thyme for the last 2 min.

~14 min
120 mlCognacor bourbon · ½ cup
120 mlHeavy cream½ cup
2 tspSoy sauce
to tasteSalt & pepper

Reduce

Brandy in, scrape fond, cook until nearly dry. Add cream and soy; cook until it holds as one mass. Season. Fridge.

~8 min
115 gFresh foie grastwo 1 cm slabs · or pâté, skip sear
to tasteSalt & pepperliberally
½ tspVegetable oil

Sear

Small skillet, oil smoking. 30 s a side. Drain on paper towel.

1 min

Split

Stir foie fat into duxelles. Halve each foie slab horizontally. Chill duxelles, foie and beef ≥30 min.

30 min
1 sheetPhyllo dough
115 gProsciuttopaper-thin · ¼ lb

Layer

Phyllo on double plastic wrap (60×30 cm). Shingle prosciutto, leaving 5 cm top and bottom. Spread duxelles over.

Roll

Beef on bottom edge, foie slices on top. Roll tight using the wrap. Re-wrap, twist ends. Chill ≥30 min.

30 min
for dustingFlour
400 gPuff pastryall-butter, thawed · 14 oz

Roll out

Rectangle at least 10 cm wider than the beef roll on its short side.

½Egg, beatenfor sealing

Wrap

Unwrap; foie side up on pastry edge. Egg-wash 15 cm above, roll, seam under. Fold, trim and seal ends. Chill ≥30 min.

30 min
½Egg, beatenfor glazing
to tasteFlaky sea saltMaldon

Bake

220 °C / 425 °F, foil-lined sheet. Egg-wash, score, salt. Bake to 43 °C rare / 49 °C medium-rare at the centre. Rest 10 min.

35–45 min
1 bunchChivesfinely minced
to tasteFlaky sea salt

Carve

Loosen from foil, move to board. Slice off ends, then carve with a serrated knife. Chives and salt on the cut faces.
