Lamb Shanks with Mustard and Mint

Serves 4 Prep 15 min Cook 2 hr Total 2¼ hr Equipment Dutch oven or braiser · Tongs · Ladle
2 TbspOlive oil
4Lamb shanksabout 1.4 kg · 3 lb
to tasteKosher salt & pepper

Sear

Oven to 180 °C / 350 °F. Oil smoking in the pot. Season shanks; brown on all sides.

~8 min
2Shallots
4 clovesGarlic

Mince

Peel and finely mince both, about 3 Tbsp shallot and 4 tsp garlic.

240 mlDry white wine1 cup

Deglaze

Stir in shallots and garlic for 15 s, then add wine at once. Boil 1 min.

~1 min
480 mlChicken or beef stocklow-sodium · 2 cups
2 TbspWorcestershire sauce
2 TbspWholegrain mustard
½ bunchFresh mintleft on the stem

Braise

Add stock, Worcestershire and mustard; scrape the pan. Lay mint on top, cover, into the oven until meat falls off the bone.

2–2½ hr
to tasteSalt & pepper

Reduce

Shanks to a platter. Simmer sauce on the hob to about 360 ml (1½ cups). Skim the fat. Season.

½ bunchFresh mint

Chop

Pick the leaves and chop roughly.

Serve

Ladle sauce over the shanks and scatter with mint.
