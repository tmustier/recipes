Spatchcocked Roast Chicken with Quick Jus

Serves 6 Prep 15 min Cook 50 min Total 1 hr 5 min, plus optional 3–24 hr dry brine Equipment Kitchen shears · Wire rack · Rimmed baking sheet · Foil · Small saucepan · Fine-mesh strainer · Instant-read thermometer
1Large chicken1.8–2.3 kg · 4–5 lb

Flatten

Oven to 230 °C / 450 °F, rack upper-middle. Cut out the backbone with shears and keep it. Press the breastbone flat.

2 tspFresh herbschopped, any mix · optional

Stuff

Gently loosen the skin over breast and thighs without tearing. Push herbs underneath evenly.

15 gKosher salt1 Tbsp · weigh other salts
½ tspBlack pepper
1 tspBaking powderoptional, for crisper skin

Season

Mix and sprinkle over skin and cavity. Skin up on a rack over a foil-lined sheet, breasts centred. Optional: fridge uncovered 3–24 hr.

1 TbspNeutral oil

Roast

Rub oil over the skin. Roast to 66 °C in the breast near the bone and 80 °C at the thigh joint.

~45 min
1 TbspNeutral oil
1Chicken backbonefrom Flatten, cut in 2.5 cm pieces

Brown

While the chicken roasts: oil shimmering in a small pan, high heat. Stir often until well browned.

~3 min
1Onionroughly chopped
1Carrotpeeled, roughly chopped
1 ribCeleryroughly chopped

Soften

Stir often until the vegetables begin to brown.

~3 min
1Bay leaf
240 mlDry vermouth or sherry1 cup
240 mlWater1 cup

Simmer

Add bay, vermouth and water; scrape the pan. Simmer 20 min. Strain, then boil down to 80 ml (⅓ cup).

~27 min
1 tspSoy sauce
45 gUnsalted butter3 Tbsp
2 tspLemon juice
to tasteSalt & pepper

Whisk

Off the heat, whisk in soy, butter and lemon. Season.

Carve

Rest the chicken 5 min under loose foil. Carve and serve with the hot jus.
